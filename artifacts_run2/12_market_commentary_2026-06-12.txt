COLTON VANCE SECURITIES — DESK MARKET COMMENTARY
12 June 2026. Internal, non-confidential.

UK industrials were mixed this week. Northshore Marine Holdings plc rose on a
contract award in the offshore support segment; the company is unrelated to the
Northshore Industrial group of companies despite the shared name, a point that
continues to cause confusion on the desk.

Elsewhere, Sundial Resources Ltd gained on firmer potash pricing. Eastvale
Chemical Company was unchanged ahead of its capital markets day.

This commentary is distributed to all trading staff and contains no
confidential transaction information.
